ABOUT THE PROJECT.

THE INTERNET FILES is an independent investigative project focused on documenting interesting stories, internet mysteries, and digital discoveries. Our goal is to present information clearly, examine credible sources, and encourage people to think critically. We distinguish verified facts from unconfirmed claims and respect people's privacy throughout our research.
